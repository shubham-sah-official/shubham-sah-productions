FAQ

Practical answers before you book.

How far in advance should we book a wedding?

Peak season dates (November–February and most destination weekends) are often held 9–14 months ahead. Off-peak and weekday ceremonies can be confirmed later. A date is only reserved after a signed agreement and retainer.

Do you travel outside Mumbai?

Yes. We regularly work across India and fly for destination weddings and music videos. Travel and stay are quoted transparently after we know the city and crew size.

How long does a wedding film take to deliver?

Highlights typically land in 6–8 weeks; full films in 10–14, depending on the season. Same-day edits are a separate, scheduled service.

Can we book photography and film together?

That is our favourite way to work. One briefing, one colour language, and stills that never fight the cinematography.

What does a music production package include?

Production, recording, mix and master can be booked as a stack or separately. Distribution is optional once the master is approved. We will listen to demos before quoting so the scope is honest.

Are newborn sessions safe?

Yes. We never force poses, we work around feeds, and a parent stays in the room. Sessions are kept short and warm.

How do payments work?

Weddings: retainer to hold the date, balance before the first function. Studio and portrait work: 50% to schedule, remainder on delivery. Invoices are GST-compliant.
