About the house

Welcome to Shubham Sah Productions.

We specialize in music production, recording, mixing and mastering, wedding cinematography, photography, music videos and cinematic films — all under one roof.

Mission

Make work that still feels true in ten years.

Our mission is to deliver audio and picture that exceed expectation — not by adding more, but by listening harder, lighting with patience, and protecting the feeling you walked in with.

We are a small crew on purpose. You will know our names. You will not be handed to a rotating roster of strangers on the day that matters.

Our story

Started with a camera, a cheap interface, and too many late nights.

Shubham Sah Productions began as a one-person practice in 2018 — covering intimate Mumbai weddings by day and mixing independent artists after midnight. The studio grew the way good records do: slowly, with the right people.

  • 2018 First destination wedding film in Goa. First paid mix for an indie EP.
  • 2020 Opened a treated recording room in Andheri and added a dedicated photography team.
  • 2023 Crossed 100 wedding films and began distributing original music for signed and independent artists.
  • 2026 Three departments, one shared grade and sonic signature, work across India and overseas.
